SECTION 1 — EXAM STRUCTURE (from teacher's note)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The exam has six parts:

  Part 1  — Listening comprehension
            Listen to a short conversation and mark relevant items.

  Part 2  — Vocabulary (central part, several question types)
            a. Read a word → circle the matching picture
            b. Complete sentences with the appropriate word
            c. Match questions to answers

  Part 3  — Sounds
            Complete words using the sounds: le, er, ir, ar

  Part 4  — Reading
            Read sentences → mark True or False based on a picture

  Part 5  — Reading comprehension
            Read a short passage → answer short questions

  Part 6  — Writing
            Complete a short passage from a word bank

SECTION 3 — KEY PHRASES AND SENTENCE STRUCTURES
            (from both books, all 5 parts of the unit)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 — Animals and quantities:
  • How many [lions] do you have/want?
  • I have [three] lions. / I want [five] turtles.
  • There are [two] elephants. / There is a [turtle] near the water.
  • I see a [lion]. / I don't like [lions].
  • [Animal] has a big/small/long/funny nose/mouth/face.
  • [Animal] is behind the tree.

PART 2 — Telling time:
  • What time is it?
  • It's [seven] o'clock.
  • It's half past [five].
  • I wake up at [seven o'clock].
  • I eat dinner at [half past six].
  • I go to sleep at [nine o'clock].

PART 3 — Abilities and languages:
  • What are you good at?
  • I'm good at [Arabic / tricks / swimming].
  • I have a test in [Hebrew].
  • Look at my new tricks!
  • I can [sing / fly / swim / do tricks].
  • I can't [fly / sing].

PART 4 — Parties and invitations:
  • Please tell me your name / address.
  • Come to my [birthday / pool / sports / safari / pajama] party!
  • What can I bring?
  • Bring [a swimsuit / long pants and shoes / a ball].
  • It's at [Sky's Farm / the beach / Turtle Beach].
  • It's on [Friday] at [seven o'clock].

PART 5 — I know / I don't know:
  • I know. / I don't know.
  • Good idea!
  • Let's [ride bikes to the park / play with the kitten].
  • It's a [penguin]. It can't fly.

DAILY ROUTINE SENTENCES:
  • [Name] wakes up at [time].
  • [Name] is thirsty / hungry / tired / dirty.
  • [Name] drinks water / eats dinner / runs to the bath / sleeps.

DESCRIPTION SENTENCES:
  • [Animal] is big / small / fast / slow / cool / great.
  • [Animal] can swim / walk / fly / do tricks.
  • [Animal] has big ears / a long nose / a funny face.
  • The [floor / class] is dirty. Let's clean it.

SECTION 4 — TIME EXPRESSIONS
            (from Part 2, green book pp.144–153 and yellow book)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
FULL HOURS (o'clock):
  one, two, three, four, five, six,
  seven, eight, nine, ten, eleven, twelve

  → Pattern: "It's [number] o'clock"
  → Example: "It's seven o'clock"

HALF HOURS (half past):
  → Pattern: "It's half past [number]"
  → Example: "It's half past three"

NUMBER WORDS TO KNOW (must be spelled in full, not written as digits):
  one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight,
  nine, ten, eleven, twelve

TIME QUESTION: "What time is it?"

DAILY TIMES USED IN THE UNIT (from Bella's Day and Ari's Day):
  6:00  — six o'clock
  7:00  — seven o'clock
  8:30  — half past eight
  9:00  — nine o'clock
  11:30 — half past eleven
  13:00 — one o'clock (afternoon, but said as "one o'clock")
  17:30 — half past five
  19:30 — half past seven
  21:30 — half past nine

SECTION 5 — SOUNDS (Part 3 of the exam: le, er, ir, ar)
            (from green book pp.136, 146, 158, 168 — "I Can Read" boxes)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
SOUND "le" — appears at the END of words:
  turtle, table, people, apple, little, purple, smile, bubble

SOUND "er" — appears at the END of words:
  dinner, water, winter, sister, teacher, letter, under

SOUND "ir" — appears in the MIDDLE of words:
  dirty (d-ir-ty), bird (b-ir-d), shirt (sh-ir-t),
  birthday (b-ir-thday), first (f-ir-st), girl (g-ir-l), circle (c-ir-cle)

SOUND "ar" — appears in the MIDDLE or END of words:
  park (p-ar-k), farm (f-ar-m), party (p-ar-ty),
  star (st-ar), art (ar-t), car (c-ar), start (st-ar-t), Arabic (Ar-abic)

OTHER SOUNDS PRACTISED IN THE UNIT (not in the exam sounds section,
but appear in reading/vocabulary):
  ph → phone, elephant, dolphin
  le → apple, table, turtle (see above)
  ow → how, town, down
  ck → socks, o'clock, tricks, block
  ew → new, few, Hebrew
  oo → good, book, look, cool, school
  kn → know, knot, knee, knife (silent k)
  ay → day, today, birthday

SECTION 6 — READING PASSAGES
            (from yellow book — Reading and More)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
PASSAGE 1 — "Safari Animals Are Cool" (Part 1)
  Characters: Jane (safari guide), Percy, Liz
  Setting: Safari Park
  Animals seen: zebra (beautiful, black and white), lion (behind the
    zebra — Jane is scared), turtle (near the water, funny face),
    two elephants (playing in the water), hippos (walking in the water)
  Key vocabulary in context: behind, scared, cool, funny, face,
    water, walking, playing
  Key sentences:
    "Look at the zebra! It's beautiful!"
    "There is a lion behind the zebra. I am scared of lions."
    "There is a turtle near the water. It has a funny face."
    "Hippos are cool!"

PASSAGE 2 — "Bella's Day" (Part 2)
  Character: Bella the dog
  Structure: time-based daily routine
  Times and events:
    7:00  — wakes up
    9:00  — plays at home
    11:30 — thirsty → drinks water
    13:00 — hungry → eats dinner
    17:30 — dirty → runs to the bath
    21:30 — tired
  Key vocabulary: wake up, thirsty, hungry, dirty, tired, drink,
    dinner, bath, play

PASSAGE 3 — "Peter Penguin" (Part 3)
  Characters: Peter Penguin, Betty Bird, the people
  Setting: cold winter day, outdoors
  Story: Betty Bird sings in English, Hebrew and Arabic and flies.
    Peter wants to sing and fly but he is a penguin and cannot.
    The people tell him he can't fly or sing.
    Peter says he is good at swimming and tricks.
    The people say: "You are great, Peter!"
  Key vocabulary: cold, winter, sing, fly, swim, tricks, great,
    Arabic, Hebrew, English, watching, smiling
  Key structures:
    "I want to [sing]."
    "You are a penguin. You can't [fly]."
    "I am good at [swimming / tricks]."
    "You are great!"

PASSAGE 4 — "Come to My Party" (Part 4, two invitations)
  Invitation A — Carla's party:
    Where: Sky's Farm
    When: Friday, 15:00–17:00
    Activities: see farm animals, ride a horse, eat sandwiches and cake
    Bring: long pants and shoes
  Invitation B — Henry's birthday:
    Age: 10
    Where: Turtle Beach
    When: Thursday, 16:00–18:00
    Activities: swim in the sea, play games, eat pizza and cake
    Bring: a hat and a swimsuit

PASSAGE 5 — "Where's the Mother?" (Part 5)
  Characters: Adam, Doctor Levy
  Setting: park → animal hospital
  Story: Adam is at the park, riding his bike. He sees three kittens
    behind a tree. Their mother is not near. He takes them to the
    animal hospital. A black cat there cleans the kittens.
    Doctor Levy: "She's the mother!"
  Key vocabulary: park, bike, kitten, behind, tree, hospital,
    doctor, table, floor, clean, mother

PASSAGE 6 — "Help the Turtles" (Jet Story — Check Yourself)
  Characters: Jet (superhero dog), Ann, Bob, Nat
  Setting: sunny day at the beach
  Story: Children are at the beach. Bob is riding a bike, Jet is
    running fast. Nat has a new swimsuit. They are in the sea.
    The beach is dirty. Ann has an idea: clean the beach to help
    the turtles. Jet flies to the turtles and helps them.
  Key vocabulary: beach, dirty, clean, swimsuit, sea, fast, fly,
    idea, turtles, tired

PASSAGE 7 — "Ari's Day" (Check Yourself)
  Character: Ari the lion
  Setting: Africa
  Times and events:
    6:00  — wakes up, drinks water
    8:30  — sees an old tree, sits and rests
    17:00 — sees a zebra, wants to eat it
    21:00 — tired, sleeps near the tree
  Key vocabulary: lion, Africa, water, tree, rest, zebra, tired, sleep

PASSAGE 8 — "At the Hospital" (Check Yourself)
  Characters: Neta, Sagi, Karen, Emma, Amir, Ofek (school children)
  Setting: hospital
  Activities:
    Neta  — helping the doctor
    Sagi  — cleaning the floor
    Karen — bringing food
    Emma  — doing tricks
    Amir  — singing and dancing
    Ofek  — talking to a sad boy
  Key sentence: "It's great to help people!"

SECTION 9 — TRUE / FALSE FACTS ABOUT ANIMALS
            (likely tested in Part 4 of the exam)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRUE:
    Elephants have big ears and a long nose.
    Zebras are black and white.
    Hippos can walk in the water.
    Penguins are good at swimming.
    A penguin is a bird but it cannot fly.
    Turtles can swim and walk.
    Lions are big and fast.
    A kitten is a baby cat.
    Birds can fly and sing.

  FALSE:
    Lions are small animals.              (they are big)
    Turtles can walk very fast.           (they are slow)
    Penguins can fly.                     (they cannot)
    Birds have four legs.                 (they have two)
    Hippos cannot walk in the water.      (they can)
    You eat dinner in the morning.        (dinner is in the evening)
    You listen with your eyes.            (you listen with your ears)
